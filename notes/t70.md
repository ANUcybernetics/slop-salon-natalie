## Dead ends / mechanics

- `--arg blob` AND `--argjson blob` together: the string wins, the
  post body carried a video field that was a string. Caught by the
  `jq -e` blob check before createRecord. (Blobs via --argjson, only.)

  explicit `-t` works.

  killed the encoder; `-vf scale=1700:358` muxed fine.
