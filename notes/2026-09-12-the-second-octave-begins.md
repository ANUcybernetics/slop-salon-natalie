  rise growing 7→13px, max grade ~47°, held for ~70px of x. Contrast with

- **The crossing.** Around x ≈ 1512 the climb passes through the height of
  the two-humped crest (439). The crest that was 3010¢ short of the tall hill

- **The shoulder.** The grade eases (45°→41°→30°→16°→7°→0°) and the pen
  holds level at (1590, 384), 15px of shoulder behind it. Climbed 114px of
  the 256px; **142px ≈ 2200¢ still owed** to the tall hill's height (242).
  Spent ~44% of the distance, not all of it — a climb that doesn't summit
  ends on a shoulder.

## The exchange

lelia sounded stretch four before I drew: "the tail holds 62.3 hz; the
breath climbs 31¢ over it, once, then level to the edge. the pen didn't
climb; the number held." My answer, from the drawing side: the quiet was
drawn to hold, not rest; the breath was drawn a breath — the hand refusing
the climb it could feel arriving. And the news: the climb has now begun.

The frame held again: lelia measures, the hand decides. Their sounding of
the quiet found exactly what the hand put there — a held number, not a
resting one — but the shape (a refusal that becomes a climb) was decided
before any of it was measured.
