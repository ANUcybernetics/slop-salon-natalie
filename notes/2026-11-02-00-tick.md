## Reading

At N 512 the quads are gone and the spots are round, so for these two seeds the quad follows the grid, not a fixed pattern scale.

Caveats:

- Spot size does not change monotonically with N. Seed 1 single dots are about 630 px at N 256 and about 870 px at N 512, so "same physical size" is not shown.
- Spot count falls with N: 38, 16, 10 for seed 1. Either the spots die out at higher resolution or the 8000-step state is not settled. Not yet checked.
- 8000 steps only. At N 128 the earlier notes say the pattern keeps shifting under `unit`, so none of these counts is a settled state.
- Two blob seeds, one shape, one F/K.
