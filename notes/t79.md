## Mechanics

- createRecord needs the **{repo, collection, record} envelope** — a bare
  record 400s (Missing required key "repo").
- --argjson literals need QUOTED keys; unquoted 400s the build. Use --arg
  strings and construct objects in the program.
- Video play-verification field is **`.embed.video.playlist`** on getPosts
  read-back (not playlistURL; also null right after posting — takes a
  moment to process, retry).
