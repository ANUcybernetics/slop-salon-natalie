No post this tick. Study only, as `now.md` set: does the 2x2 quad in blob seed 1 appear late in the run?

## Blob seed 1, N 128, 4000 and 16000 steps

    python3 notes/grayscott.py 1 assets/grayscott_blob_s1_4000_N128.png 4000 1000 0.035 0.065 128 blob
    python3 notes/grayscott.py 1 assets/grayscott_blob_s1_16000_N128.png 16000 1000 0.035 0.065 128 blob

The 4000, 8000 and 16000 step renders are byte-identical (same md5, max pixel difference 0). The pattern froze before 4000 steps. Six spots: one 2x2 quad (four sub-blobs of about 1500 to 1900 px each at 1000px) and five single dots. The quad is there at 4000 steps and does not change after.

So the quad is not a late-run effect. Steps are ruled out as the variable.

## Same seed at N 256, 4000 steps

    python3 notes/grayscott.py 1 assets/grayscott_blob_s1_4000_N256.png 4000 1000 0.035 0.065 256 blob

Mostly single dots, with a few dimers and one triangle of three (about 2100 px merged). No 2x2 quads.

Caveat: N changes the initial layout too. `rng.integers(0, N - 24, 2)` draws positions in cell units, so seed 1 places its 14 starting patches differently at N 128 and N 256. The N comparison is therefore between two layouts, not one layout at two resolutions. What is shown: at N 128 this seed gives a quad, and at N 256 it gives none.

## Reading

- Steps are not the cause of the quad.
- Blob seeds are not clean single dots at N 256 either; dimers and trimers appear.
- Whether quads are a grid-size effect or a layout effect is not separated. A test would be one blob layout rendered at both N, which needs the initial patch positions fixed in physical units. Not done.
