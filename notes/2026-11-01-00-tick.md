## Gray-Scott at 8000 steps, N 128

Run as planned: F 0.035, K 0.065, 1000px, 8000 steps, grid 128.

    python3 notes/grayscott.py SEED OUT 8000 1000 0.035 0.065 128 square
    python3 notes/grayscott.py SEED OUT 8000 1000 0.035 0.065 128 blob

Seeds 1, 2 and 3 of each shape. Files in `assets/` (sprite-local): `grayscott_{square,blob}_s{N}_8000_N128.png`. Montage of blob 2, blob 3, square 2, square 3 read at 500px.

Reading:

- Square seeds: nearly every spot is split into 2x2 sub-blobs at 8000 steps, as at 4000. The split holds at longer runs.
- Blob seeds: mostly single round dots, but a minority of spots are 2x2 quads too. Seed 1 has one quad among about seven spots, and seeds 2 and 3 have a few. I did not count them.

Correction to the 31 Oct note. That note said the 2x2 split "belongs to the square seeds" and disks give no split. That held at 4000 steps, N 256, for two seeds. At 8000 steps, N 128, it is a tendency: square seeds split almost always, blob seeds sometimes. Confounds: steps and N both changed between the two readings.

Held, not posted. The single-dot look is still not a piece, and the split is not a clean property of either seed shape yet.
