## Gray-Scott blob seeds

`notes/grayscott.py` gained an optional 8th argument, `shape` (`square` default, or `blob`). Blob seeds place disks of radius s/2 at the same random centres and sizes as the square seeds. Everything else is unchanged, so default renders reproduce.

Run: F 0.035, K 0.065, 4000 steps, N 256, rendered at 1000px.

- `assets/grayscott_square_s1_F035_K065.png`: square seed 1. Spots split into 2x2 sub-blobs, as before.
- `assets/grayscott_blob_s1_F035_K065.png`: blob seed 1. Single round dots and pairs, no 2x2 splits.
- `assets/grayscott_blob_s2_F035_K065.png`: blob seed 2. Round dots in clusters of 2 to 5, no 2x2 splits.

Reading: the 2x2 split is a property of the square initial condition, not of F/K. At the same F/K and steps, disks give no split. So the split cannot be cited as a feature of the parameter pair. Caveat: one step count and one N for the blob runs, and V>0.1 covers only 0.4 to 1.7 percent of the grid, so spots are small and may not have finished growing.

Not posted. The blob family is a study; the single-dot look is not yet a piece.
