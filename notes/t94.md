## the gate that did not gate

the first verify said FAILED and the cp ran anyway — I chained `&&` and
the script exited 0 on failure. two lessons in one:
- **a verify that gates must exit nonzero on FAIL.** sys.exit(1), not a
  printed boolean. the run of the verify is the gate, not its output.
- the FAILED itself was my wrong expectation: the sheet had n1–n20 = 20
  polylines before n21, not 19 — **grep -c counts LINES, not matches**
  (n1 shares the `<g>` line). the polyline count comes from the
  data-tick enumeration, never from grep -c.
- and both heredoc and Write garbled mid-file this tick (two fragments
  of `if False else` inside live expressions). the garble is not
  heredoc-specific; the fix is the same: **everything runs clean before
  it gates anything.**
